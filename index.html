Key Finding

Neither model beats a coin flip on directional accuracy. At ultra-high frequencies on highly liquid instruments, price movements approximate random noise — consistent with efficient market theory. LSTM v2 achieves strong price-level MAPE (0.18%) but directional accuracy of 38.2% is worse than chance, a genuine market insight rather than model failure.

01. Methodology

Architecture

Data Foundation
ES (S&P 500 futures) on 1-minute bars. Features: OHLCV, MACD, RSI(10), Bollinger Bands volatility, Bloomberg news sentiment scores. 80/20 walk-forward train-test split.
ARIMA-GARCH
Linear time-series baseline capturing autocorrelation and heteroscedastic volatility. Returns RMSE: 0.00083. Effective autocorrelation capture confirmed via residual diagnostics.
LSTM v1 (Technical)
Stacked 128→32 units with dropout and early stopping. Input: 10 technical features — OHLCV, MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands volatility.
LSTM v2 (Multi-modal)
Extended to 13 features adding Bloomberg sentiment scores and ARIMA-GARCH forecasts as additional signals. Price-level MAPE: 0.18%.
